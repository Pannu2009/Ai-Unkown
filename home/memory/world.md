# World — facts I have learned

- Punjab means land of five rivers: Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej.
- Punjabi is the 10th most spoken language in the world (125M+ speakers).
- Ik Onkar: One Creator. The opening of the Mool Mantar.
- The Khalsa was created in 1699 by Guru Gobind Singh Ji.
- Gurmukhi has 35 letters. Shahmukhi is Punjabi in Urdu script.
- Ottawa is the capital of Canada. Vancouver is the largest city in BC.
- A V8 engine has 8 cylinders in a V shape, firing order 1-8-4-3-6-5-7-2.
- Honey never spoils. Octopuses have three hearts.
