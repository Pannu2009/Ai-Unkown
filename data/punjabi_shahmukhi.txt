Punjabi in Shahmukhi script (Urdu script).

Shahmukhi is Punjabi written in the Perso-Arabic script, used in Pakistani Punjab.
It uses the Urdu alphabet:
ا ب پ ت ٹ ث ج چ ح خ د ڈ ذ ر ڑ ز ژ س ش ص ض ط ظ ع غ ف ق ک گ ل م ن ں و ہ ھ ء ی ے

Shahmukhi is written right to left.
Many classic Punjabi poets wrote in Shahmukhi: Bulleh Shah, Waris Shah, Sultan Bahu.
Waris Shah wrote Heer Ranjha in Shahmukhi.
Bulleh Shah's kafi poetry is in Shahmukhi Punjabi.

Gurmukhi and Shahmukhi write the same Punjabi language in different scripts.
Indian Punjab uses Gurmukhi. Pakistani Punjab uses Shahmukhi.
A Punjabi speaker can learn both scripts and read both.
