Sikh history and Gurbani.

Sikhism was founded by Guru Nanak Dev Ji, born in 1469 at Talwandi, now Nankana Sahib in Pakistan. He taught Ik Onkar, that there is one universal Creator.

The ten Sikh Gurus are: Guru Nanak Dev Ji, Guru Angad Dev Ji, Guru Amar Das Ji, Guru Ram Das Ji, Guru Arjan Dev Ji, Guru Hargobind Ji, Guru Har Rai Ji, Guru Har Krishan Ji, Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji, and Guru Gobind Singh Ji.

Guru Arjan Dev Ji compiled the Adi Granth and built the Harmandir Sahib, the Golden Temple, in Amritsar. He was martyred in 1606.

Guru Hargobind Ji wore two swords, miri and piri, for temporal and spiritual power. He built the Akal Takht.

Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji gave his life in 1675 defending the right of Hindus to practice their faith. He is called Hind di Chadar, the shield of India.

Guru Gobind Singh Ji founded the Khalsa on Vaisakhi in 1699 at Anandpur Sahib. He gave the Five Ks: Kesh, Kangha, Kara, Kachera, and Kirpan. He declared the Guru Granth Sahib Ji as the eternal Guru.

The three pillars of Sikhism are Naam Japo, meditate on God's name; Kirat Karo, earn an honest living; and Vand Chakko, share with others.

Seva is selfless service. Langar is the free community kitchen where all sit together as equals. Pangat is the row in which all sit together, kings and beggars alike.

The Mool Mantar: Ik Onkar Sat Naam Karta Purakh Nirbhau Nirvair Akaal Moorat Ajooni Saibhang Gur Prasad. It means: One Universal Creator God. Truth is His Name. Creative Being Personified. Without Fear. Without Hatred. Image of the Undying. Beyond Birth. Self-Existent. By the Guru's Grace.

From Japji Sahib: Jap. Aad Sach Jugaad Sach. Hai Bhee Sach Nanak Hosee Bhee Sach. Chant and meditate. True in the primal beginning. True through the ages. True here and now. O Nanak, forever and ever True.

Guru Nanak taught: there is no Hindu, there is no Muslim. All humans are equal. He rejected the caste system.

The Guru Granth Sahib Ji contains the bani of the Sikh Gurus and saints like Kabir, Farid, and Ravidas. It is written in Gurmukhi script.

Vaisakhi in 1699: Guru Gobind Singh Ji asked for five heads. Five beloved ones, the Panj Pyare, stepped forward. He gave them amrit and made them the Khalsa.

The Khalsa code: never cut hair, carry the kirpan, live truthfully, protect the weak, never bow to tyranny.

Banda Singh Bahadur established the first Sikh rule. Maharaja Ranjit Singh built the Sikh Empire with Lahore as its capital in the early 1800s.

The Golden Temple's four doors face all directions, welcoming everyone. Its lower floor stands below ground level, teaching humility.

Sikhs greet each other with Sat Sri Akaal, meaning True is the Timeless One. Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh is the Khalsa greeting.

Ik Onkar is the first phrase of the Guru Granth Sahib Ji. It means One Creator. This is the foundation of Sikh belief.

Guru Ram Das Ji founded the city of Amritsar. Guru Amar Das Ji made langar compulsory before meeting the Guru.

The martyrdom of the four Sahibzade, the sons of Guru Gobind Singh Ji, is remembered every December. The younger two, Zorawar Singh and Fateh Singh, were bricked alive at Sirhind for refusing to convert.

Sikh history is a history of standing against injustice, protecting the innocent, and serving humanity without distinction.
